ABOUT GLOBALWEATHER

Weather information, made simple.

GlobalWeather is a weather information website designed to help people explore current weather conditions and forecasts for locations around the world.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make weather information easy to understand, accessible and useful. Whether you are checking the weather in your hometown or exploring another country, we aim to give you a simple way to find the information you need.

What You Can Explore

  • Current weather conditions.
  • Hourly weather forecasts.
  • Daily weather forecasts.
  • Temperature, humidity and wind information.
  • Weather information for searched locations.

Our Weather Data

Weather information on this website is provided by WeatherAPI.com . Data availability and forecast coverage depend on the provider.

Our Commitment

We aim to present weather information clearly and responsibly. Weather conditions can change, so always check official local guidance during severe weather.
