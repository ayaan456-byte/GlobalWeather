Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to common questions about GlobalWeather.

1. What is GlobalWeather?

GlobalWeather is a website that helps you check current weather conditions and forecasts for locations around the world.

2. How do I check the weather?

Enter a city or location in the search box on the homepage and press the search button.

3. Can I check weather in other countries?

Yes. You can search for supported locations around the world. Coverage depends on our weather data provider.

4. Can I use my current location?

Yes. Select the current location button and allow your browser to access your location.

5. Is the weather information always accurate?

Weather information is provided by WeatherAPI.com. Forecasts are estimates and actual conditions may change.

6. Can I change Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Yes. When weather results are displayed, use the temperature unit switch to change between Celsius and Fahrenheit.

7. Is GlobalWeather free to use?

You can access the website without creating an account. Any future features or changes will be described on the website.

8. Where does the weather data come from?

Weather data is provided by WeatherAPI.com.

9. How can I share feedback?

Visit our Contact page to prepare a message for us.

10. What should I do during severe weather?

Follow official local weather warnings and instructions from your local authorities.
