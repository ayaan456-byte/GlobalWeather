Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Introduction

GlobalWeather respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use our website.

2. Information You Provide

When you search for weather, the location you enter is sent to our weather service through our website's server function so that weather information can be retrieved.

3. Location Permission

If you choose the current location feature, your browser may ask for permission to share your device location. This permission is optional. You can search for a location manually instead.

4. Weather Data Provider

We use WeatherAPI.com to retrieve weather information. Your searched location may be processed by that provider. Please review its terms and privacy information .

5. Cookies and Storage

Our website may use technical storage or caching to support its operation and improve performance. Hosting infrastructure may also process basic technical request information.

6. Contact Form

Our contact form prepares an email using your device's email application. It does not directly submit or store messages on our website.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the website and keep private configuration details, such as API credentials, on the server rather than in public website code.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is a general weather information service and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when website features or practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, please visit our Contact page.
